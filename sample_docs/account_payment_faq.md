# Account and Payment FAQ

## Account Management

### How do I reset my password?
1. Go to login.company.com
2. Click **Forgot Password**
3. Enter your registered email address
4. Check your inbox for a reset link (valid for 24 hours)
5. Click the link and create a new password

### How do I update my email address?
Email address changes require identity verification. Contact support@company.com with:
- Your current email address
- Proof of identity (government-issued ID)
Changes are processed within 2 business days.

### Can I have multiple accounts?
Each user is permitted one account. Multiple accounts may be merged or deactivated at our discretion.

### How do I delete my account?
Submit an account deletion request at account.company.com/delete. Your data will be permanently deleted within 30 days in accordance with our Privacy Policy and applicable regulations (GDPR, DPDP Act 2023).

## Payment Methods

### Accepted Payment Methods
- Credit cards: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, RuPay
- Debit cards: All major Indian bank debit cards
- UPI: PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, BHIM
- Net banking: 50+ Indian banks supported
- Wallets: Paytm Wallet, Amazon Pay
- Buy Now Pay Later: ZestMoney, LazyPay, Simpl
- EMI: Available on credit cards and select debit cards (3, 6, 9, 12 months)

### Is my payment information secure?
Yes. We are PCI DSS Level 1 compliant — the highest security standard for payment processing. We do not store your full card number on our servers. All transactions are encrypted using TLS 1.3.

### Why was my payment declined?
Common reasons for payment failure:
- Insufficient funds or credit limit
- Card expired or blocked by issuing bank
- 3D Secure authentication failed
- Transaction flagged by fraud detection

If your payment fails, try:
1. Using a different payment method
2. Contacting your bank to authorise the transaction
3. Clearing browser cache and retrying

### What is the EMI process?
EMIs are available for orders above ₹3,000. During checkout:
1. Select **EMI** as payment method
2. Choose your bank and tenure (3/6/9/12 months)
3. Enter your card details
4. The EMI is processed by your bank — we receive the full amount upfront

### How do I get a GST invoice?
If you have a GST number, enter it during checkout or in your account settings under **Business Profile**. GST invoices are auto-generated and available in **Orders** → **Download Invoice**.

## Wallet and Credits

### What are Store Credits?
Store credits are issued for returns, referrals, and promotional campaigns. They:
- Never expire
- Can be used for any order
- Cannot be transferred to another account or converted to cash

### Referral Program
Earn ₹200 store credit for each friend who signs up and places their first order using your referral link. Your friend also receives ₹100 off their first order. Find your referral link at account.company.com/refer.
