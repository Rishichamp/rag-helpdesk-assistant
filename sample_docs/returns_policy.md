# Returns and Refunds Policy

## Overview

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. This document outlines our returns and refunds policy for all products sold on our platform.

## Return Window

You may return most items within **30 days** of the delivery date for a full refund. The 30-day window begins on the date your order is marked as delivered by the carrier.

## Eligible Items

The following items are eligible for return:
- Physical products in original, unopened packaging
- Defective or damaged items (regardless of packaging condition)
- Items that do not match the product description on our website

## Non-Eligible Items

The following items **cannot be returned**:
- Digital products (software licenses, ebooks, online course access) — all sales are final
- Perishable goods (food, flowers, plants)
- Customised or personalised items
- Intimate or sanitary goods for hygiene reasons
- Hazardous materials

## How to Initiate a Return

1. Log into your account at account.company.com
2. Navigate to **Orders** → select the order
3. Click **Request Return** and select a reason
4. Print the prepaid return shipping label (physical items only)
5. Drop the package at any authorised carrier location

Returns are typically processed within **5–7 business days** of receiving your item.

## Refund Timeline

Once we receive and inspect your return:
- Credit/debit card: 3–5 business days to reflect in your account
- Store credit: Issued immediately upon return approval
- Bank transfer: 7–10 business days

## Exchange Policy

We offer direct exchanges for defective items or sizing issues. To request an exchange, follow the same return process and select **Exchange** instead of refund.
