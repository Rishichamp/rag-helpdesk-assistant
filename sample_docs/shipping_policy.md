# Shipping and Delivery Policy

## Domestic Shipping (India)

### Standard Shipping
- Delivery time: 5–7 business days
- Cost: Free on orders above ₹500; ₹49 flat fee on orders below ₹500
- Coverage: Available in 19,000+ pin codes across India

### Express Shipping
- Delivery time: 1–2 business days
- Cost: ₹149 flat fee regardless of order value
- Coverage: Available in metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Pune)

### Same-Day Delivery
- Available in select areas of Mumbai, Delhi NCR, and Bangalore
- Orders must be placed before 12:00 PM IST
- Cost: ₹249 flat fee

## International Shipping

We currently ship to the following countries:
- **South Asia**: Sri Lanka, Bangladesh, Nepal (5–10 business days)
- **Southeast Asia**: Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand (7–14 business days)
- **Rest of World**: 14–21 business days

International shipping costs vary by weight and destination. Customs duties and taxes are the responsibility of the recipient.

## Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you will receive:
1. A shipping confirmation email with your tracking number
2. SMS updates at key delivery milestones
3. Real-time tracking at track.company.com or via our mobile app

## Delivery Attempts

Our carrier will attempt delivery up to 3 times. After 3 failed attempts:
- The package is held at the nearest carrier facility for 7 days
- After 7 days, the package is returned to us
- A full refund minus shipping costs is issued

## Address Changes

Address changes are possible only if the order has not yet been dispatched. Contact support@company.com within 2 hours of placing your order.

## Damaged in Transit

If your item arrives damaged:
1. Take photos of the damage and packaging
2. Report within 48 hours at support@company.com
3. We will send a replacement at no cost or issue a full refund
